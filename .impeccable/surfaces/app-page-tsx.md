## Direction contract

THESIS: A night field woven like a pagne, where the points of light are the data themselves — households connected, accounts still active. The page refuses the category default it would otherwise land on: a dark hero with one neon accent followed by a grid of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep indigo ground (#070E1A to #0C1A2E), a regular matrix of small light points whose density varies, warp-and-weft hairlines at 1px in low-opacity light. One saturated weft colour, amber-coral, and nothing else coloured. One geometric sans (Jost) for everything: display in tracked uppercase at 96-140px, body in the same family. No cards: full-bleed horizontal bands (registers) stacked and separated by hairlines, each opened by a left selvedge rail. Repeated identical gauges carry per-country data. Red is reserved for the single anomalous value.

STORY: The visitor understands that this student both secures systems and makes data speak; believes it because figures, method and quality controls are exposed rather than claimed; and acts by opening the UEMOA case study or making contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed night. A 56px left selvedge rail runs the full height, carrying vertical "DEFILER" type and a thin amber thread whose fill is the real scroll position. Left two-thirds: the name in two giant tracked uppercase lines, a hairline, then the double claim in two lines of body. Right third: the woven light field, its density resolving into an aperture that holds the portrait. Bottom of the viewport, along a hairline: availability chip, the primary action as a filled amber rectangle, and three tracked figures (L3, 09, 05).

FORM: Pagne wax / West African weaving, candidate 6 of the ordered grounded list, assigned by the roll; seed key 351cbb82. The user's pinned night materials bind; the weave supplies topology, controls and ritual.
